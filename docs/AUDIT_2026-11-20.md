## What was changed (this release)

1. **Administrative geography.** Boundary version 2026-11-20 contains the **1,541 communes** from OpenStreetMap, assigned to the 69 wilayas and checked against the commune lists of Loi n° 26-06. Wilayas and the national outline are unions of communes, so the levels nest. See `scripts/processing/build_admin.py` and the boundary method page.
2. **Reference product.** *Algeria Precipitation (CHIRPS v3.0)*, `aeo-dza-precipitation-chirps3` v1.0.0:
   - COGs for Algeria and each wilaya;
   - statistics tables (CSV and Parquet) for nation, wilayas and communes;
   - 1991–2020 normals, anomalies and trends;
   - a manifest with checksums, provenance, a method page and QA tests.
3. **Analyze** (`/analyze/`):
   - select Algeria, a wilaya or a commune, and up to 4 areas to compare;
   - period: calendar year, agricultural year, season or month, over any range of years;
   - outputs: time series, anomalies, Theil–Sen and Mann–Kendall trend, a choropleth map with click selection, and CSV, SVG and PNG exports.
4. **Regions** (`/regions/`): profiles for 69 wilayas and 1,541 communes, built from the published files.
5. **Catalog:**
   - Algeria-ready products are separated from the upstream source library;
   - new statuses `experimental` and `under-validation`; "in preparation" is shown as *Planned*, "archived" as *Deprecated*;
   - a `product` block with a manifest is validated at build;
   - the download builder follows country → wilaya → commune → variable → period → format.
6. **Map:** commune boundaries, and annual precipitation and anomaly layers with a year slider.
7. **QA:**
   - `tests/python` covers statistics unit tests and product QA (coverage, CRS, completeness, units, aggregation reconciliation, checksums);
   - `tests/js` checks that the browser trend code matches Python;
   - CI runs both, and the deploy workflow requires them.

## Decisions and their reasons

- **CHIRPS v3.0 rather than v2.0:** v2.0 production ends after December 2026; v3.0 runs from 1981 to the present at the same 0.05° resolution, with CC BY 4.0.
- **Commune statistics yes, commune rasters no:** at about 5.5 km, many Tell communes cover fewer than four cells. Statistics are published with a support class; spatial subsets are not.
- **Static first:** all analysis runs in the browser on precomputed tables (about 10–15 MB per product). No server is needed.

## Blockers, costs and permissions

- **GitHub Pages source setting** (account owner): set *Settings → Pages → Source* to **GitHub Actions**.
- **Large files:** the GACED30 Algeria clip (783 MB) needs a GitHub Release created by the account owner, or external object storage (for example Cloudflare R2: free up to 10 GB, with egress-free reads).
- **Data server availability:** the CHIRPS server rate-limited or blocked this environment for a period during processing. The fetch script resumes from its cache and retries.
- **No paid infrastructure is required** for the current release.

## Next phases (not yet built)

1. Temperature (ERA5-Land needs a Copernicus CDS account, or a CHIRTS-type source) and SPI-1/3/6/12 from the precipitation product (gamma fit on 1991–2020, with zero-precipitation handling).
2. Vegetation condition (MODIS/VIIRS NDVI anomalies). The Terra MODIS end on 1 December 2026 requires a VIIRS continuity plan.
3. Cropland: GACED30 area per wilaya and commune per year (the clips exist locally); cropland × drought exposure.
4. Surface water: JRC GSW statistics per wilaya.
5. French and Arabic interfaces.
