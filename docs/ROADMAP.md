# Roadmap and future features

These are **plans**, not existing capabilities. Nothing here should be described as available until it is released.

## Near term (next releases)

- Complete organisation setup and first public deployment (see `DEPLOYMENT.md`).
- Research the remaining candidates listed in `SOURCE_AUDIT.md` (ESA CCI Land Cover, Copernicus Global Land Service, FAO WaPOR, GRACE/GRACE-FO, dams databases, CHIRTS, VIIRS Black Marble, Hansen Global Forest Change).
- Record successors as providers transition: CHIRPS v3; VIIRS replacements for Terra MODIS products (acquisitions end 1 December 2026); corrected SPEIbase release.
- A 58-wilaya and 48-wilaya aggregation of the boundary layer, so that historical statistics can be joined correctly.
- Illustrative agro-ecological regions (Tell and northern plains, High Plateaus, Saharan Atlas, Saharan oases, southern desert) as a clearly labelled non-administrative layer, with sources.

## AEO product families (status: in preparation)

Released only after method notes, reproducible code, validation and review:

- AEO Cropland Extent — Algeria
- AEO Agricultural Land-Use Change
- AEO Winter Cereal Distribution
- AEO Fallow Land and Cropping Activity
- AEO Agricultural Drought Indicators
- AEO Greenhouse Agriculture Monitoring
- AEO Climate and Vegetation Time Series

## Possible future capabilities

- Search and browse by wilaya and watershed; data download by region and period.
- Automated updates of climate summaries (scheduled GitHub Actions within free limits).
- Seasonal agricultural condition and drought early-warning summaries.
- Read-only research data API (static JSON/Parquet first).
- Jupyter notebooks and reproducible analysis environments.
- Institutional data contributions with documented data-sharing agreements.
- DOI-based publication of AEO products (e.g. Zenodo integration).
- French and Arabic versions of the site after reviewed translations.
- Training workshops and educational resources.
- Contributor attribution (all-contributors style) with consent.
- Sustainable funding and maintenance arrangements, published transparently.
