# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Algeria Earth Observatory welcomes contributors of every background, institution, career stage, language and nationality. We commit to making participation a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive; critique data and methods, not people.
- Credit others' work, data and ideas accurately.
- Be honest about uncertainty, limitations and conflicts of interest.
- Respect data licences, privacy and the confidentiality of unpublished work.
- Use welcoming language. Contributions in English, French or Arabic are welcome.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Plagiarism or misrepresentation of data provenance or results.
- Deliberately submitting fabricated or misleading data or metadata.
- Political, ethnic or regional hostility. Discussion of environmental and scientific topics is welcome; attacks on groups are not.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and events — and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report concerns to the maintainers. Until a dedicated project address exists, open an issue stating only that you need to raise a conduct concern privately (without details), and a maintainer will contact you through a private channel. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban participants, depending on severity. Decisions are explained to the person concerned.
