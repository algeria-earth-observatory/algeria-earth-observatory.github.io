## Cite the producer, not the catalog

AEO is a catalog. When you use an external dataset, the citation belongs to its **original producer**. Each dataset page shows the provider's recommended citation and a **Copy citation** button. Where a data paper exists, cite it as well (listed under *References*).

A complete data citation contains:

- authors or producing organisation;
- year of publication of the version you used;
- dataset title **and version**;
- publisher or repository;
- persistent identifier (DOI) when one exists;
- access date for datasets that are updated continuously.

Example (ERA5-Land monthly, as recommended by the Copernicus Climate Change Service):

> Muñoz Sabater, J. (2019): ERA5-Land monthly averaged data from 1950 to present. Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S) Climate Data Store (CDS). DOI: 10.24381/cds.68d2bb30 (Accessed on DD-MMM-YYYY)

## Licences and attribution

Licences are set by producers and differ between datasets — for example CC BY 4.0 (attribution required), CC0 or public domain (no conditions), ODbL (attribution and share-alike for databases), or custom terms such as the HydroSHEDS licence. The AEO website licence (CC BY 4.0 for text, MIT for code) **does not** apply to the data. Read the licence linked on each dataset page before redistributing data.

Maps you publish must carry the attribution string the provider requests, for example “Source: EC JRC/Google” for Global Surface Water or “© OpenStreetMap contributors” for OSM-derived boundaries.

## Citing AEO-distributed files

For files with status **Available** (distributed by AEO), cite the AEO record, which gives the version and the underlying sources:

> Algeria Earth Observatory (2026). Algeria national and wilaya boundaries (69 wilayas), OpenStreetMap snapshot 2026-11-19, version 2026-11-19. Data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. https://algeria-earth-observatory.github.io/data/aeo-dza-admin-boundaries/

AEO does not yet mint DOIs. When AEO products are archived in a DOI-issuing repository, the DOI will be added to the record and becomes the preferred identifier.

## Acknowledging AEO

If the catalog helped you find or prepare data, an acknowledgement is welcome but optional, for example: “Dataset discovery and documentation: Algeria Earth Observatory (https://algeria-earth-observatory.github.io).”
