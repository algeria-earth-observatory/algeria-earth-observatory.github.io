# Algeria's administrative geography (wilayas), status at 2026-11-19

Researched 2026-11-19. Legal facts below are taken from the official *Journal officiel de la République algérienne* (JORADP, French translation). All four PDFs were downloaded from joradp.dz and read in full for the relevant articles.

## 1. Official count: 69 wilayas and 1,541 communes (since April 2026)

| Step | Legal text | Journal officiel | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base law | Loi n° 84-09 du 4 février 1984 relative à l'organisation territoriale du pays | (1984) | 48 wilayas |
| 2019 reform | **Loi n° 19-12 du 11 décembre 2019** modifiant et complétant la loi n° 84-09. New Art. 3: "La nouvelle organisation territoriale du pays comprend cinquante-huit (58) wilayas et mille cinq cent quarante-et-une (1541) communes." | **JO n° 78 du 18 décembre 2019**, https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2019/F2019078.pdf | 58 wilayas |
| 2021 names | **Décret présidentiel n° 21-117 du 22 mars 2021** complétant le décret n° 84-79 du 3 avril 1984 fixant les noms et les chefs-lieux des wilayas: 49 Timimoun, 50 Bordj Badji Mokhtar, 51 Ouled Djellal, 52 Béni Abbès, 53 In Salah, 54 In Guezzam, 55 Touggourt, 56 Djanet, 57 El M'Ghaier, 58 El Meniaa | **JO n° 22 du 25 mars 2021**, https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2021/F2021022.pdf | names/codes 49–58 |
| 2026 reform | **Loi n° 26-06 du 4 avril 2026** modifiant et complétant la loi n° 84-09. New Art. 3: "Le nouveau découpage territorial du pays comprend soixante-neuf (69) wilayas et mille cinq cent quarante-et-une (1541) communes." Adds Art. 52 bis 10 to 52 bis 20 (one new wilaya each, defined by its list of communes) and amends the commune lists of 10 existing wilayas (Art. 7, 9, 11, 16, 17, 18, 21, 30, 32, 36 of law 84-09). Art. 5 renames the base law "relative au découpage territorial du pays". | **JO n° 25 du 5 avril 2026**, https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2026/F2026025.pdf (Arabic: https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-arabe/2026/A2026025.pdf) | 69 wilayas |
| 2026 names | **Décret présidentiel n° 26-206 du 25 mai 2026** complétant le décret n° 84-79: 59 Aflou, 60 Barika, 61 El Kantara, 62 Bir El Ater, 63 El Aricha, 64 Ksar Chellala, 65 Aïn Ouessara, 66 Messaad, 67 Ksar El Boukhari, 68 Bou Saâda, 69 El Abiodh Sidi Cheikh (each with chef-lieu of the same name) | **JO n° 40 du 3 juin 2026**, https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2026/F2026040.pdf | names/codes 59–69 |

Notes:
- The commune total stays at 1,541 in both reforms: new wilayas are formed by regrouping existing communes, so no commune boundary was created by these laws.
- Communes forming each new 2026 wilaya (from law 26-06, Art. 3): Aflou 12 communes; Barika 8; El Kantara 5; Bir El Ater 4; El Aricha 4; Ksar Chellala 6; Aïn Ouessara 10; Messaad 8; Ksar El Boukhari 21; Bou Saâda 23; El Abiodh Sidi Cheikh 7. (Mapping articles to names: law 26-06 Art. 52 bis 10..20 in the same order as decree 26-206 numbers 59..69; the first commune listed in each article is the chef-lieu named in the decree.)
- Mother wilayas are not named in the law text itself (the law amends the commune lists by article number). From commune names they are: Laghouat (Aflou), Batna (Barika), Biskra (El Kantara), Tébessa (Bir El Ater), Tlemcen (El Aricha), Tiaret (Ksar Chellala), Djelfa (Aïn Ouessara, Messaad), Médéa (Ksar El Boukhari), M'Sila (Bou Saâda), El Bayadh (El Abiodh Sidi Cheikh). This is an inference from the commune lists, consistent with press reporting, not a quotation.
- **Transition:** amended Art. 54 of law 84-09 (law 26-06, Art. 4): during set-up, the authorities of the mother wilayas keep all prerogatives for the new wilayas, and "Les walis des wilayas mères transfèrent progressivement, au plus tard, le 31 décembre 2026" these prerogatives to the new wilayas. 2026 budgets of mother wilayas continue to be executed by the mother wilaya (Art. 55, 57). So as of late 2026 the 69-wilaya division is legally in force, but administrative transfer may not be complete until 31 Dec 2026. Statistics for 2026 and earlier will generally be reported on 48 or 58 wilayas.
- Dating: the reform is sometimes called "the 2025 reform" because, per press reports (not checked against an official communiqué), the Council of Ministers approved the creation of the 11 wilayas in November 2025. The legal act is **2026**: law 26-06 of 4 April 2026. Use "law 26-06 (2026)" in site labels.
- Secondary confirmation (press): Horizons, 6 Apr 2026, https://www.horizons.dz/2026/04/organisation-territoriale-lalgerie-passe-officiellement-a-69-wilayas/ ; conformepro.dz summary of JO 25/2026.

No openly licensed boundary dataset found that represents the 58-wilaya (2019/2021) or the 69-wilaya (2026) configuration. Because both reforms only regroup existing communes, a 58- or 69-wilaya layer can in principle be built by dissolving a 1,541-commune layer (COD-AB admin 2 / gbHumanitarian ADM2, both CC BY IGO) using the commune lists in law 19-12 and law 26-06, but this would be an AEO-derived product and commune names must be matched carefully. Not checked: OpenStreetMap's current admin_level=4 relations (Overpass queries timed out on 2026-11-19). The separate AEO record `aeo-dza-admin-boundaries` states that an OSM snapshot of 2026-11-19 contains 69 wilayas (ODbL); that claim was not independently verified in this research.

## 3. Recommendation for the site's wilaya layer label

Label any of the above layers explicitly as "48 wilayas (pre-2019 configuration)" with source and vintage, and state that Algeria has officially had 58 wilayas since law 19-12 (2019; names fixed March 2021) and 69 wilayas since law 26-06 (JO n° 25, 5 April 2026; names fixed by decree 26-206, JO n° 40, 3 June 2026). Do not call a 48-unit layer "current wilayas".
